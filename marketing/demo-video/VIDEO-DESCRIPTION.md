# FeDril product demonstration

Compliance readiness work can become fragmented across spreadsheets, inboxes, and shared folders. This demonstration shows how FeDril helps a fictional contractor organize readiness activities in one No-CUI workspace.

Follow Northstar Precision Systems, a fictional organization created only for this video, as its team:

- reviews current readiness signals;
- opens a high-priority, source-backed obligation;
- makes ownership and an overdue remediation task visible;
- reviews non-sensitive evidence metadata already associated with the obligation;
- reviews the No-CUI boundary;
- examines tenant-scoped activity; and
- opens a reviewable leadership readiness summary.

FeDril supports a repeatable compliance-management process by centralizing visibility into ownership, evidence metadata, gaps, and remediation. The product does not replace qualified legal, compliance, or assessment judgment. This demonstration uses no customer records, production data, CUI, FCI, uploaded document content, or real contract information.
