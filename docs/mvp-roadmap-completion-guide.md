## Executive Interpretation

## Why Planned And Completed Can Coexist

| Field | Question answered | Valid values | Transition authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Roadmap Classification | Is the material roadmap outcome present in the approved product or governed operating model? | Implemented; Partially implemented; Planned; Do not claim | Current behavior, focused passing tests, governed operating evidence, and authoritative external evidence where applicable |
| Task Status | Does this specific task have sufficient completion evidence? | Completed; Partially Completed; Blocked; Not Started; Not Verified; External/Human Gate; Not Applicable | Task-specific deliverable and evidence package |

A `Planned + Completed` combination is valid when the completed work is preparatory, narrower than the roadmap outcome, historical but not freshly verified, or governed only for a limited pilot scope. It is invalid only when current evidence proves the complete roadmap outcome and the classification has not been synchronized.

`Complete` is not a roadmap classification. The normalized target is `Implemented`. The workbook phrase `Partially implemented foundation` should be retained as quoted source wording or normalized to `Partially implemented`; it should not become a fifth roadmap status.

## Status And Classification Transition Rules

### Roadmap transition gate

Change `Planned` to `Implemented` only after all material layers for the roadmap outcome are proven: domain rules, application orchestration, persistence and migrations where applicable, thin API composition, user-visible workflow where applicable, tenant isolation, server-side RBAC, append-only audit behavior, No-CUI enforcement, focused passing tests, smoke/regression evidence, and required operating or external evidence.

Use `Partially implemented` when a meaningful subset is proven but any material acceptance criterion, layer, migration, failure path, approval, evidence period, or external dependency remains. Use `Do not claim` for unsupported, prohibited, expired, or independently controlled assertions.

### Task and story completion gate

A task becomes `Completed` only when its observable deliverable and task-specific evidence source, locator, type, verification date, result, and limitation are recorded. A software story becomes `Completed` only when every canonical task is complete and every applicable acceptance criterion has passing positive, invalid, denied, cross-tenant, rollback/no-side-effect, persistence, audit, and UI-state evidence.

A research or governance story requires a governed artifact, qualified reviewer, findings, disposition, owner, and date. An external-assurance story requires the actual authoritative external outcome. If evidence proves only a subset, use `Partially Completed`. If completion and non-start are both unproven, use `Not Verified`.
