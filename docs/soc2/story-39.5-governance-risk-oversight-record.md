3. Use the following proposed ordinal anchors consistently over a management-selected twelve-month planning horizon. Likelihood 1: exceptional conditions are needed and no credible current path is established by available evidence; 2: a credible path exists or supporting controls are uncertain; 3: observed events, active exposure or a readily usable path support recurrence. These are ordinal judgment categories, not annual probabilities. Impact 1: contained/reversible internal disruption within approved commitments, no material sensitive-data/customer consequence; 2: material remediation, interruption or contractual/customer harm; 3: severe data-boundary, business-continuity, fraud or legal/regulated consequence.
