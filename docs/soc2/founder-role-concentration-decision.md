## Conflict And Compensating-Control Decision

Founder concentration creates self-review, management-override, key-person and evidence-integrity risks. Until staffing changes, FeDril will use these minimum safeguards:

1. FR-01 may collect evidence and make management decisions but may not label their own review independent.
2. A competent external security reviewer must validate material security classifications, containment results and closure evidence.
3. Qualified counsel must address legal/privacy/contract notification questions when triggered by verified facts.
4. A qualified independent CPA must review SOC 2 scope, criteria mapping, route and readiness and must conduct any examination.
5. Protected evidence must retain source, date, integrity/version, collector, reviewer, access and decision history.
6. Destructive or externally consequential actions still require target-specific authorization, rollback and verification; this standing role appointment is not blanket authorization.

While FR-01 remains the sole entity, management self-review may preserve metadata, document decisions and prepare drafts, but it cannot satisfy independent review. Artifact inspection remains blocked until the protected-workspace and restricted-inspection gates are met. Containment may not be declared complete until a future independent validator reviews the applicable evidence.
