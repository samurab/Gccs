### D3 — Conditional Security / Forensic Questions And Chain Of Custody

The future Security/forensic intake must define the exact authorized object and
hash, provenance question, time boundary, isolated tools, operator, permitted
outputs, stop conditions and preservation method. Chain-of-custody fields are:
opaque evidence ID; source; collector; acquisition method; date/time and time

Questions include whether provenance and integrity are supportable; what
minimal inspection is necessary; which exposure paths and credentials are

and residual copies can later be validated independently.

advice, do not supply case-specific deadlines, and their citation does not
establish that a protocol or all-inclusive investigation was completed.
