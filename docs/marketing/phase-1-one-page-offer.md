# Phase 1: One-Page Offer

Document type: customer-facing offer draft for qualified discovery prospects.

Status: draft. Founder, product, security, and counsel review required before public use.

## Headline

Replace one fragile compliance readiness spreadsheet with a controlled No-CUI readiness workspace.

## Who It Is For

GCCS is for small government contractors and subcontractors that need a practical way to organize readiness work across contract metadata, source-backed obligations, ownership, evidence metadata, reports, and audit history.

Best fit:

- Small federal prime or subcontractor.
- Preparing for CMMC-related readiness, contract review, flow-down review, or evidence organization.
- Currently using spreadsheets, email, shared drives, or informal tracking.
- Can complete the pilot with synthetic, redacted, or non-sensitive data only.

## The Problem

Readiness work often fails because the tracker is not connected to the source requirement, evidence, owner, status, or report artifact. When a prime, customer, advisor, or internal leader asks for proof, the team has to rebuild the story manually.

## The Pilot

### 30-Day Guided Readiness Pilot

GCCS will guide one No-CUI readiness workflow from setup to report artifact:

1. Confirm No-CUI pilot boundary.
2. Set up company profile and contract metadata.
3. Attach or review relevant clauses.
4. Generate and review source-backed obligations.
5. Assign owners and update workflow status.
6. Link allowed evidence metadata.
7. Generate a Compliance Status, CMMC Readiness, or Evidence Package artifact where applicable.
8. Review findings and decide whether to convert.

## Required Boundary

The current GCCS MVP is No-CUI / compliance management only. Do not upload, paste, import, attach, store, or process real CUI, classified information, ITAR/export-controlled data, credentials, payroll records, SSNs, health data, sensitive incident details, or other prohibited sensitive data.
