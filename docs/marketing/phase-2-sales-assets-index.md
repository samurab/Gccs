## Required Sales Package Boundary

Every Phase 2 sales asset must preserve these boundaries:

- FeDril is No-CUI / compliance management only in the MVP.
- Real CUI is prohibited.
- Classified information, ITAR/export-controlled data, sensitive government-furnished information, credentials, payroll, SSNs, health data, disability data, sensitive incident details, and unrestricted security logs are prohibited.
- FeDril does not provide legal advice, accounting advice, labor determinations, CMMC certification, assessor determinations, contracting-officer determinations, or government endorsement.
- Reports and readiness labels are workflow guidance, not pass/fail or assessment-success determinations.
- Source-backed content remains reviewable and subject to qualified expert review where required.

## Recommended Usage Sequence

1. Send the security and No-CUI pages before or immediately after the first serious demo request.
2. Use the 10-slide deck for the first structured sales conversation.
3. Run the minimum demo script with synthetic, redacted, or non-sensitive data only.
4. Share the sample readiness report PDF as a leave-behind.
5. Use the pricing page to propose the paid 30-day guided pilot.
6. Route terms and disclaimer language to counsel before real sales, order forms, or public launch.

## Phase 2 Exit Criteria

Phase 2 sales assets are usable for controlled customer discovery only when:

- The deck, demo script, pricing page, security page, No-CUI policy, sample workflow, and sample report are internally reviewed.
- Customer-facing claims have been checked against the current application UI, API enforcement, permissions, and tests.
- Counsel has reviewed public-facing terms, disclaimers, pricing language, and pilot terms before real sales.
- The demo environment uses only synthetic, redacted, or non-sensitive data.
- The sales process has a documented way to stop or redirect prospects who require real CUI handling.
- The paid pilot offer is clear: scope, price, conversion credit, exclusions, and decision deadline.

## Hidden Risks

- Sales assets can drift from product controls if updated independently from onboarding, upload warnings, and support scripts.
- A sample report can be mistaken for a compliance determination if disclaimers are removed.
- Prospects may ask for real CUI upload during a demo; the demo script must stop and restate the No-CUI boundary.
- Pricing can become misleading if pilot scope, conversion credit, refund policy, or subscription terms are not matched in the order form.
- Counsel review is a dependency, not a formality, because these assets touch regulated compliance expectations.
