# Phase 1: Founder Action Checklist

Document type: execution checklist for getting from MVP to first qualified pilot.

## Before First Discovery Call

- Review the locked offer in `phase-1-lock-the-offer.md`.
- Confirm the demo tenant uses synthetic, redacted, or non-sensitive data only.
- Confirm No-CUI acknowledgement appears in `Contracts` and `Evidence`.
- Confirm the demo path uses current tabs: `Contracts`, `Obligations`, `Evidence`, `Reports`, and `Settings`.
- Prepare the one-page offer.
- Prepare the qualification scorecard.
- Prepare the discovery call script.

## During Discovery

- Start with No-CUI and no-certification boundary.
- Identify one painful workflow.
- Confirm current tracker pain.
- Confirm internal owner.
- Confirm allowed data.
- Score the prospect.
- Avoid solving the problem for free during discovery.
- Close for pilot scope review if qualified.

## Before Offering A Pilot

- Score is at least 13, or founder intentionally approves a conditional 9-12 score.
- Customer accepts No-CUI boundary.
- Customer names an internal owner.
- Pilot workflow is narrow.
- Success criteria are written.
- Exclusions are stated.
- Pilot fee and conversion credit are understood.
- Counsel-reviewed language is used for any real order form or agreement.

## Offer Revision Triggers

Revise the offer only after patterns appear.

Revise ICP if:

- Three or more qualified conversations show a different buyer owns the pain.

Revise pilot scope if:

- Three or more qualified buyers ask for the same narrower workflow.

Revise pricing if:

- Qualified buyers accept the pain but consistently reject the fee.

Do not revise the offer because one unqualified prospect asks for custom features, real CUI handling, free consulting, or certification guarantees.
