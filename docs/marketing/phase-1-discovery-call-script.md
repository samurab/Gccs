# Phase 1: Discovery Call Script

Document type: 30-minute founder-led discovery call script.

## Call Objective

Determine whether the prospect has a painful readiness workflow that can be solved with a paid No-CUI guided pilot.

## Call Agenda

| Time | Segment | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3 min | Boundary and context | Set No-CUI and no-certification expectations. |
| 3-10 min | Current workflow | Understand the spreadsheet, owner, evidence, and report pain. |
| 10-18 min | Impact and urgency | Determine whether the pain is worth paying to solve. |
| 18-24 min | Pilot fit | Confirm data boundary, internal owner, and workflow scope. |
| 24-30 min | Close | Ask for paid pilot, demo, or explicit no-fit decision. |

## Opening Script

"Before we talk product, I want to be clear about the boundary. GCCS is currently a No-CUI compliance management and readiness workflow tool. It does not provide legal advice, CMMC certification, assessor determinations, or government endorsement. For a pilot, we only use synthetic, redacted, or non-sensitive data."

## Discovery Questions

1. What readiness workflow is hardest to manage right now?
2. Where is it tracked today?
3. Who owns the tracker?
4. What happens when someone asks for proof?
5. Which contract, subcontract, review, or readiness process would be best for a 30-day pilot?
6. What evidence exists, and where does it live?
7. Can we represent the evidence with non-sensitive metadata only?
8. Who would be the internal pilot owner?
9. What would make this pilot worth $750?
10. What would make you convert after the pilot?

## Qualification Signals

Strong fit:

- They can name a specific workflow.
- They have a current tracker or spreadsheet.
- Evidence exists but is hard to connect to obligations.
- They accept the No-CUI boundary.
- They can name an internal owner.
- They understand reports are workflow guidance only.

Weak fit:

- They speak only in general compliance terms.
- No owner exists.
- No workflow is urgent.
- They ask for free consulting.
- They need custom integrations before value is proven.

Disqualify or redirect:

- They require real CUI handling now.
- They want certification or legal conclusions.
- They want to paste sensitive incident, payroll, health, credential, or export-controlled data into the pilot.

## Close Options

### If Qualified

"This sounds like a fit for the 30-day guided readiness pilot. The scope would be one No-CUI workflow, one internal owner, allowed evidence metadata, and a report artifact at the end. The pilot is $750 and credited toward the first annual subscription if you convert. Are you comfortable moving to a pilot scope review?"

### If Interested But Not Ready

"The next useful step is to identify one workflow and confirm whether it can be run with synthetic, redacted, or non-sensitive data. Without that, a demo will be too abstract."

### If Not A Fit

"Based on what you need, GCCS is not the right MVP fit today because the current product does not handle real CUI or certification decisions. I would rather be clear about that now than overpromise."
