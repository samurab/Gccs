## 3. Buyer Discovery

Target: 20 conversations before broad launch.

| Metric | Target | Current |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small contractor conversations | 10 | 0 |
| Consultant/advisor conversations | 5 | 0 |
| MSP/security provider conversations | 3 | 0 |
| APEX/SBA ecosystem conversations | 2 | 0 |
| Total discovery conversations | 20 | 0 |

### Required Discovery Questions

- How do you track contract obligations today?
- Where does evidence live today?
- Who owns readiness status?
- What happens when a prime, customer, assessor, or internal reviewer asks for proof?
- Which spreadsheet, folder, or process breaks first?
- What compliance task do you repeat every month or quarter?
- What would make a readiness workspace worth paying for?
- What would make you refuse to use it?
- Can you use synthetic, redacted, or non-sensitive data during a pilot?

### Repeated Pain Signals To Track

- Evidence is hard to find.
- Clause-to-task ownership is unclear.
- Readiness status is manual or stale.
- Reports take too long to build.
- Subcontractor flow-downs are inconsistent.
- CMMC readiness is confused with certification.
- The team relies on one person.
- Existing tools are too generic or too complex.

## 4. Prospect Qualification

### Qualified Prospect Criteria

A prospect counts as qualified only if they meet all required criteria:

- U.S. government contractor, subcontractor, advisor, or MSP serving that market.
- Has a live compliance workflow.
- Has a named owner for compliance readiness.
- Has a repeated obligation, evidence, report, task, or audit-history problem.
- Accepts No-CUI pilot boundaries.
- Can participate in a guided pilot within 30-60 days.

### Disqualification Criteria

Disqualify or defer prospects that:

- Require CUI handling in the MVP.
- Require formal CMMC certification or assessment claims.
- Require legal, labor, accounting, or contracting determinations.
- Require FedRAMP, GovCloud, SSO, SCIM, or enterprise procurement before pilot.
- Cannot identify a specific workflow GCCS would improve.

### Common Objections To Prepare For

| Objection | Risk | Required Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| We already use spreadsheets. | Buyer does not see enough workflow pain. | Show ownership, audit history, source-backed obligations, and report generation. |
| Can this certify CMMC? | Claims/liability risk. | State that GCCS tracks readiness workflows and evidence metadata; it does not certify compliance. |
| Can we upload CUI? | Product posture risk. | State that the MVP is No-CUI only and requires synthetic, redacted, or non-sensitive data. |
| We need a consultant, not software. | Service-heavy buyer. | Offer guided pilot and advisor-friendly workflow, not self-serve-only onboarding. |
| We need integrations. | Expansion distraction. | Validate the core workflow first; defer integrations until repeated demand. |

## 6. Pilot Conversion Gate

Do not count a prospect as a pilot unless they commit to:

- A named internal owner.
- A kickoff call.
- One contract or synthetic workflow.
- Non-sensitive or synthetic pilot data.
- Weekly feedback during the pilot.
- A decision meeting at the end of 30 days.
- A paid pilot fee or written procurement step.

## 7. Phase 1 Completion Criteria

Phase 1 is complete when all of the following are true:

- ICP is narrowed to one primary buyer segment.
- Product promise is stable across website, deck, demo, and outbound email.
- Claims/disclaimer language is reviewed before external use.
- Demo can be completed in under 10 minutes.
- 20 discovery conversations are completed and logged.
- At least 5 prospects agree to view a demo.
- At least 2 prospects agree to a paid pilot or concrete procurement step.
- Top 5 objections have tested responses.
- Pricing hypothesis has real buyer feedback.

## Dependencies

- Final customer-facing claims review before public marketing.
- Legal or contracting advisor review before paid pilots.
- Demo environment with synthetic-only data.
- No-CUI onboarding and upload guardrails active during pilot use.
- Support path for suspected prohibited-data incidents.
- Source-backed content governance for obligation examples.

## Hidden Risks

- Discovery calls can create false confidence if they do not test payment or procurement.
- Advisors may like the concept but block product adoption if they see it as service displacement.
- Buyers may say they need CMMC help but actually need basic evidence and ownership tracking.
- Pricing feedback is weak unless tied to a specific pilot offer.
- A single enthusiastic buyer should not redefine the ICP unless the pain repeats across multiple prospects.
