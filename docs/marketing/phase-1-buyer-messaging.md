# Phase 1 Buyer Messaging

This document turns the locked offer into market-facing language by buyer type. Use it for landing pages, outbound email, sales decks, demo scripts, and pilot conversations.

## Messaging Rules

- Lead with workflow pain, not fear.
- Use "readiness," "tracking," "evidence," "tasks," "reports," and "audit history."
- Avoid "certify," "guarantee," "official determination," "legal advice," "CMMC assessor," and "government approved."
- State the No-CUI posture before evidence upload, demo setup, pilot onboarding, and support intake.
- Keep claims specific to what GCCS does today.

## Primary Message

GCCS helps small government contractors replace scattered compliance spreadsheets with a No-CUI workspace for source-backed obligations, evidence readiness, task ownership, reports, and audit history.

## Short Message Options

- Compliance readiness tracking for small government contractors.

## Persona: Owner / Founder

### Pain

The owner is accountable for contract and customer expectations but cannot see whether compliance work is actually current.

### Message

GCCS gives you a clear readiness view across obligations, evidence gaps, owners, and reports without relying on a fragile spreadsheet.

### Proof Points

- Readiness dashboard.
- Assigned obligation owners.
- Report export.
- Audit history.
- Source-backed obligation records.

### Discovery Questions

- Who owns compliance tracking today?
- What happens when a prime asks for evidence?
- Which spreadsheet or folder is the source of truth?
- What would be painful to recreate during a review?

### Objections

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| We already have spreadsheets. | GCCS is for the point where spreadsheets stop showing ownership, source history, evidence status, and reportable readiness in one place. |
| We are too small for GRC. | GCCS is intentionally not enterprise GRC. The first offer is a guided readiness workspace for small contractor workflows. |
| We need certification help. | GCCS supports readiness tracking and evidence organization. It does not certify compliance or replace an assessor or advisor. |

## Persona: Operations / Contracts Lead

### Pain

The contracts or operations lead has to convert contract requirements into tasks, reminders, evidence requests, and status updates.

### Message

GCCS helps you turn contract requirements into owned obligations, evidence records, readiness status, and reports.

### Proof Points

- Contract metadata.
- Manual clause tagging.
- Obligation dashboard.
- Compliance calendar.
- Subcontractor flow-down tracker.
- Readiness report.

### Discovery Questions

- How do clauses become tasks today?
- How do you know evidence is current?
- Which deadlines or reporting requirements are easiest to miss?
- How do subcontractor flow-downs get tracked?

### Objections

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Clause tagging is manual. | Correct. The MVP keeps clause tagging reviewable instead of pretending automation can make unreviewed determinations. |
| We need integrations. | The pilot should prove the core workflow first: obligations, owners, evidence metadata, and reports. Integrations come after repeatable demand. |
| Our contracts vary. | GCCS is designed around source-backed obligation records and manual review, so unusual contracts can be handled without unsupported automation claims. |

## Demo Opening Script

GCCS is a No-CUI compliance management workspace for small government contractors. The goal is not to certify compliance or replace an advisor. The goal is to help a team know what obligations apply, assign the work, track evidence metadata, and produce a readiness report without running the process through spreadsheets and email.

## Pilot Positioning Script

The first pilot is a 30-day guided readiness pilot. We set up your company profile, model one contract or synthetic workflow, tag obligations manually, assign owners, attach allowed evidence metadata, and generate a readiness report. The pilot excludes real CUI, legal advice, certification decisions, and custom integrations.

## Qualification Checklist

A prospect is qualified for Phase 1 if:

- They are a small U.S. government contractor, subcontractor, MSP, or compliance advisor.
- They have a current compliance tracking workflow.
- They can name a person responsible for readiness.
- They have a recurring evidence, obligation, task, or reporting problem.
- They accept No-CUI pilot boundaries.
- They are willing to pay for a pilot or commit to a specific next step.

A prospect is not qualified if:

- They require real CUI handling immediately.
- They need a formal CMMC assessment outcome.
- They need legal, labor, accounting, or contracting determinations.
- They require enterprise procurement before trying the product.
- They cannot identify a real workflow GCCS would improve.
