# Phase 1: Offer Validation Checklist

Document type: internal checklist before moving from discovery to Phase 2 sales assets.

## Product Claim Validation

| Check | Required answer |
| --- | --- |
| Does the offer claim only implemented or clearly demo-supported workflows? | Yes |
| Are current tabs named accurately? | Yes |
| Are all No-CUI boundaries stated? | Yes |
| Are reports described as workflow guidance only? | Yes |
| Are certification, legal, accounting, labor, and government-determination claims excluded? | Yes |

## Offer Readiness

- ICP is specific.
- Poor-fit prospects are clearly identified.
- Pilot is paid by default.
- Pilot scope can be delivered without custom engineering.
- Pilot data can remain synthetic, redacted, or non-sensitive.
- Conversion credit is clear.
- Stop conditions are documented.
- Counsel review is identified before public use.

## Hidden Risks

- The buyer may equate readiness with certification.
- The customer may try to upload prohibited data.
- The pilot may expand into unpaid consulting if scope is not enforced.
- The sales deck may drift from current implementation if not checked against the app.
- Pricing may need revision after the first three to five discovery calls.
