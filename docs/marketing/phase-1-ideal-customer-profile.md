# Phase 1: Ideal Customer Profile

Document type: ICP definition for founder-led discovery.

Required boundary: target customers must be able to use synthetic, redacted, or non-sensitive data during the MVP pilot.

## Primary ICP

Small U.S. government contractors and subcontractors that manage compliance readiness through spreadsheets, shared drives, email, and informal ownership tracking.

## Best-Fit Signals

- Small business with federal or defense contracting exposure.
- Preparing for CMMC-related readiness, contract review, evidence organization, or flow-down management.
- Has a contract, subcontract, or synthetic workflow suitable for a No-CUI pilot.
- Has an internal owner who can attend kickoff and end-of-pilot review.
- Currently lacks a clean way to answer what applies, who owns it, what evidence exists, and what remains open.

## Buyer Personas

| Persona | Pain | GCCS angle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner / founder | Needs readiness visibility without becoming the compliance project manager. | Show dashboard, open obligations, owner assignment, and reports. |
| Operations lead | Tracks work across spreadsheets and people. | Show obligation work queue, status, due dates, and evidence metadata. |
| Contracts lead | Needs to connect contract clauses to follow-up work. | Show Contracts tab, attached clauses, and source-backed obligation generation. |
| Security lead | Needs evidence organization and readiness status without storing real CUI. | Show Evidence tab, No-CUI acknowledgement, metadata fields, and report artifacts. |
| Advisor / MSP | Supports multiple small contractors and needs repeatable workflows. | Position as a pilotable operating workspace, not enterprise GRC. |

## Disqualifiers

- Requires real CUI upload or processing in the MVP.
- Expects GCCS to certify CMMC compliance.
- Wants legal, accounting, labor, contracting-officer, or assessor determinations.
- Needs custom integrations before proving value.
- Cannot commit a named internal pilot owner.

## Hidden Risks

- Some buyers will say "readiness" but expect certification. Redirect immediately.
- Advisors may want multi-client features before one customer workflow is proven.
- Contractors may accidentally paste sensitive details into descriptions. Reinforce No-CUI boundaries during discovery and demo.
