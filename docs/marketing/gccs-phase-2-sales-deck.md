# FeDril Phase 2 Sales Deck

Purpose: 10-slide sales deck for founder-led discovery, pilot demos, advisor conversations, and first-customer sales calls.

Audience: small U.S. government contractors, subcontractors, MSPs, and compliance advisors.

Mandatory boundary: FeDril is No-CUI / compliance management only in the MVP. This deck must not claim legal advice, CMMC certification, assessment success, government endorsement, or permission to upload real CUI.

## Slide 1 - Compliance Readiness Tracking For Small Government Contractors

**Headline:** Compliance readiness tracking for small government contractors.

**Subhead:** FeDril helps small GovCon teams organize source-backed obligations, assign ownership, track evidence metadata, and generate current report artifacts under a No-CUI compliance management posture.

**Speaker note:** Open with the boundary. FeDril is not a certification body, legal advisor, or CUI storage product. It is a compliance operations workspace for readiness tracking.

## Slide 2 - The Problem: Compliance Work Is Scattered

**Headline:** Small contractors are asked to prove readiness, but the work is scattered.

**Key points:**

- Contract obligations live in PDFs, spreadsheets, email, and shared drives.
- Evidence exists, but teams cannot quickly prove what it supports.
- Ownership and due dates depend on manual follow-up.
- Status snapshots and evidence packages are rebuilt for every prime, customer, advisor, or internal review.
- Readiness status often depends on one person knowing where everything is.

**Speaker note:** Do not overstate regulatory risk. Focus on operational drag, stale tracking, and proof gaps.

## Slide 3 - Why Spreadsheets Break

**Headline:** Spreadsheets track lists. They do not manage compliance workflows.

**Key points:**

- No source-backed obligation history.
- Weak evidence traceability.
- No consistent audit trail.
- No structured owner, status, and review workflow.
- No safe data-handling boundary for prohibited information.

**Speaker note:** The replacement target is not enterprise GRC. The replacement target is spreadsheet-driven readiness management.

## Slide 4 - The FeDril Promise

**Headline:** Know what applies. Track what was done. Stay ready to prove it.

**Key points:**

- Turn contract and compliance requirements into owned obligations.
- Track evidence metadata without using FeDril as a CUI repository.
- Monitor readiness across obligations, calendar items, reports, and reviews.
- Preserve audit history for compliance-relevant activity.
- Keep customer-facing content source-backed and reviewable.

**Speaker note:** This is the core positioning. Avoid "automated compliance" language.

## Slide 5 - How FeDril Works

**Headline:** A simple workflow for readiness operations.

**Workflow:**

1. Set up company profile and data-handling posture.
2. Add contract metadata and attach or review clauses.
3. Generate and review source-backed obligations from mapped clauses.
4. Assign owners and update workflow status.
5. Create allowed evidence metadata and link it to obligations or controls where applicable.
6. Generate Compliance Status, CMMC Readiness, Subcontractor Compliance, or Evidence Package artifacts.
7. Review audit history.

**Speaker note:** Say "review-driven clause and obligation handling" clearly. Extraction candidates and generated obligations are not final compliance determinations without human review.

## Slide 6 - What The MVP Includes

**Headline:** Focused modules for high-frequency GovCon compliance work.

**Key points:**

- Company compliance profile.
- Contract and clause intake with review-driven clause handling.
- Source-backed obligation dashboard.
- Compliance calendar.
- Evidence metadata workspace with No-CUI upload guardrails for allowed non-sensitive evidence.
- CMMC Level 1 and Level 2 readiness tracker.
- Subcontractor flow-down tracker.
- Basic reports and audit log.

**Speaker note:** Keep this slide concrete. Do not add deferred features such as SSP builder, SPRS score calculator, AI assistant, GovCloud, or FedRAMP readiness.

## Slide 7 - Trust Boundary: No-CUI MVP

**Headline:** Built for No-CUI compliance management first.

**Key points:**

- Real CUI is prohibited in the MVP.
- Classified information, ITAR/export-controlled data, credentials, payroll, SSNs, health data, and sensitive incident details are prohibited.
- Pilot workflows use synthetic, redacted, or non-sensitive data.
- Reports are workflow guidance, not legal advice, certification decisions, or assessor determinations.
- Future CUI-ready operation requires a separately approved architecture, controls, terms, and support process.

**Speaker note:** This slide prevents dangerous buyer assumptions. Use it before discussing evidence upload or pilot onboarding.

## Slide 8 - Who FeDril Is For

**Headline:** Designed for small teams that need readiness discipline without enterprise GRC overhead.

**Best-fit buyers:**

- Small federal prime contractors.
- Small subcontractors to large primes.
- DoD suppliers preparing for CMMC-related reviews.
- 8(a), WOSB, HUBZone, SDVOSB, and SDB participants.
- MSPs, consultants, contracts admins, and compliance advisors supporting small contractors.

**Poor-fit buyers:**

- Organizations requiring real CUI handling immediately.
- Buyers seeking certification guarantees.
- Enterprises requiring SSO, SCIM, GovCloud, FedRAMP, or custom procurement on day one.

**Speaker note:** Use this to qualify. A bad-fit prospect should not drive the roadmap.

## Slide 9 - 30-Day Guided Pilot

**Headline:** Validate the workflow with one guided readiness pilot.

**Pilot includes:**

- Company profile setup.
- One contract or synthetic workflow.
- Manual clause attachment and generated obligation review.
- Evidence metadata setup using allowed data.
- Readiness dashboard walkthrough.
- Compliance Status, CMMC Readiness, or Evidence Package generation where applicable.
- End-of-pilot findings session.

**Pilot boundaries:**

- No real CUI.
- No legal advice.
- No CMMC certification or assessment determination.
- No custom integrations in the first pilot.

**Pricing hypothesis:** $500-$1,500 paid pilot, credited toward the first annual subscription if converted.

**Speaker note:** The pilot must be paid unless the prospect is a strategic design partner with referral or advisor value.

## Slide 10 - Next Step

**Headline:** Replace one fragile compliance workflow with a controlled readiness workspace.

**Call to action:**

- Schedule a 30-minute fit call.
- Select one contract or synthetic readiness workflow.
- Confirm No-CUI pilot boundaries.
- Identify the internal owner.
- Start the 30-day guided pilot.

**Closing line:** FeDril helps your team organize what applies, track who owns it, and produce readiness evidence without running the process through scattered spreadsheets.

**Speaker note:** Ask for a concrete next step: fit call, demo, pilot owner, or paid pilot decision. Do not end with a vague "stay in touch."
