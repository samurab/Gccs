# Phase 1: Pilot Offer

Document type: first paid pilot scope and qualification guide.

## Offer

30-day guided readiness pilot.

## Price

$750 flat pilot fee.

Credit the pilot fee toward the first annual subscription if the customer converts within the agreed conversion period.

## Pilot Outcome

By the end of the pilot, the customer should have one configured No-CUI readiness workflow that shows contract metadata, attached or reviewed clauses, generated obligations, owner/status tracking, allowed evidence metadata, a current report artifact, and audit history.

## Included

- Kickoff and workflow selection.
- No-CUI boundary confirmation.
- Company profile and contract metadata setup.
- Clause attachment or review.
- Generated obligation review.
- Owner assignment and status workflow.
- Evidence metadata setup using allowed data.
- Compliance Status, CMMC Readiness, or Evidence Package generation where applicable.
- End-of-pilot findings session.

## Excluded

- Real CUI upload, processing, storage, or support handling.
- Legal advice.
- Accounting advice.
- Labor determinations.
- CMMC certification or assessor determinations.
- Government determinations or endorsements.
- Custom integrations.
- GovCloud, FedRAMP, SSO, SCIM, and enterprise procurement support.

## Pilot Success Criteria

| Criterion | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Named owner assigned | Customer identifies one internal pilot owner. |
| Workflow selected | One contract or synthetic workflow is selected. |
| No-CUI boundary accepted | Customer agrees to use synthetic, redacted, or non-sensitive data only. |
| Obligations reviewed | Source-backed obligations are visible and reviewed for the pilot workflow. |
| Evidence metadata linked | Allowed evidence metadata is linked to obligations or controls where applicable. |
| Report artifact generated | Reports tab artifact is generated and reviewed as workflow guidance only. |
| Conversion decision made | Customer decides to convert, continue discovery, or stop. |

## Pilot Close

"If this workflow solves the spreadsheet problem, the next decision is whether to convert the pilot workspace into your paid subscription."
