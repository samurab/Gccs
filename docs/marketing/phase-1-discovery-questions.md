# Phase 1: Customer Discovery Questions

Document type: discovery call guide.

## Qualification Questions

1. What compliance workflow is currently most painful?
2. Where is that workflow tracked today?
3. Which contract, subcontract, review, or readiness process creates the pain?
4. Who owns the current spreadsheet or tracker?
5. Who is accountable when a prime, customer, advisor, or reviewer asks for proof?
6. Where does evidence live today?
7. Can the pilot use synthetic, redacted, or non-sensitive data only?
8. What would make a 30-day pilot worth paying for?
9. Who would attend kickoff and end-of-pilot review?
10. What would prevent you from converting after a successful pilot?

## Product-Fit Questions

- Do you need contract metadata and clause tracking?
- Do you need obligation ownership and status tracking?
- Do you need evidence metadata organization?
- Do you need report artifacts for internal or advisor review?
- Do you need audit history for who changed what and when?

## No-CUI Boundary Questions

- Can this pilot be completed without uploading real CUI?
- Can evidence be represented as synthetic, redacted, or non-sensitive metadata?
- Do users understand that FeDril reports are workflow guidance only?
- Is there any reason a user would try to paste prohibited data into notes, descriptions, uploads, support messages, or screenshots?

## Stop Or Redirect Questions

Stop or redirect if the prospect says:

- "We need to upload real CUI."
- "Can FeDril certify us?"
- "Can FeDril tell us if we legally comply?"
- "Can we paste actual incident details into the demo?"
- "We cannot assign anyone to own the pilot."

## Close Questions

- Is this problem painful enough for a paid pilot?
- Is $750 acceptable for a 30-day guided pilot with conversion credit?
- Who signs off on the pilot?
- What date should kickoff happen?
- What would count as success at the end?
